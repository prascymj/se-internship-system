# Test Cases — Scoring and Recording Interview Results During Supervision

Step-by-step test cases for the feature [6. Scoring and Recording Interview Results During Supervision](../../design/feature-list.md), referencing the acceptance criteria in [acceptance-criteria](../acceptance-criteria.md) and the flow in [user-journey](../../design/user-journey.md).

> **Note (2026-09-16):** FR-18 was revised from "supports multiple Supervising Instructors per round" to "1 Supervising Instructor per company" — the original test cases that tested the multiple-instructors-per-round case (assigning multiple instructors, setting a summary score via manual override per ~~FR-21~~/~~FR-30~~) have all been removed from this file, since FR-21/FR-30 are Deprecated and no longer within the testing scope — see the original content in *(Deprecated — FR-21/FR-30, removed from scope)*, replaced by the new FR-18 (1:1) test cases and FR-33 (online/onsite) below.

| ID | Test case name | Precondition | Steps | Expected result | AC tested | FR/NFR code | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TC-06-01 | Only 1 Supervising Instructor is responsible per company throughout the round (revised 2026-09-16) | A host company/Student has been assigned a Supervising Instructor per FR-32 | 1. Log in with an Internship Coordinator or authorized user account<br>2. Open the 3-month internship round information for that host company<br>3. Check the list of Supervising Instructors responsible | The system shows only 1 Supervising Instructor responsible for that host company throughout the 3-month round, with no other Supervising Instructor sharing responsibility at the same time | [FR-18 AC-1](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-18 | High |
| TC-06-02 | Reject score entry from a Supervising Instructor who was not assigned (revised 2026-09-16) | A host company has an assigned Supervising Instructor responsible for it (FR-32) | 1. Log in with a different Supervising Instructor account that is not the assigned one<br>2. Attempt to open the score/interview-result entry form for that company's supervision round<br>3. Attempt to save | The system rejects the save and reports no permission, since the account is not the Supervising Instructor responsible for this company | [FR-18 AC-2](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-18 | High |
| TC-06-03 | Supervising Instructor records scores per the rubric — on-site round | The Supervising Instructor has finished an on-site supervision visit to the Student at the host company | 1. Log in with a Supervising Instructor account<br>2. Open the scoring form for a supervision round whose mode is set to "on-site"<br>3. Fill in the scores for every dimension assessed per the rubric<br>4. Save | The system saves the Supervising Instructor's scores with that supervision round, also tagging it with the on-site mode | [FR-19 AC-1](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-19 | High |
| TC-06-04 | Supervising Instructor records scores per the rubric — online round (new 2026-09-16) | The Supervising Instructor has finished supervising the Student online via an online channel | 1. Log in with a Supervising Instructor account<br>2. Open the scoring form for a supervision round whose mode is set to "online"<br>3. Fill in the scores for every dimension assessed per the rubric<br>4. Save | The system saves the Supervising Instructor's scores with that supervision round successfully, the same as the on-site case, also tagging it with the online mode (without needing to meet the NFR-03/NFR-07 conditions) | [FR-19 AC-2](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-19 | High |
| TC-06-05 | Supervising Instructor records interview-form results — on-site round | The Supervising Instructor has interviewed the Student at the host company on-site (continuing from TC-06-03) | 1. Open the interview form for that on-site supervision round<br>2. Fill in the answers per the defined question set<br>3. Save | The system saves the interview-form results with that supervision round, also tagging it with the on-site mode | [FR-20 AC-1](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-20 | High |
| TC-06-06 | Supervising Instructor records interview-form results — online round (new 2026-09-16) | The Supervising Instructor has interviewed the Student online via an online channel (continuing from TC-06-04) | 1. Open the interview form for that online supervision round<br>2. Fill in the answers per the defined question set<br>3. Save | The system saves the interview-form results with that supervision round successfully, the same as the on-site case, also tagging it with the online mode | [FR-20 AC-2](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-20 | High |
| TC-06-07 | Set a supervision round as on-site (new 2026-09-16) | The Supervising Instructor begins recording a supervision round for a company they are responsible for | 1. Log in with a Supervising Instructor account<br>2. Open a new supervision round for the company they are responsible for<br>3. Select the supervision mode as "on-site" | The system links that supervision round to the host company's pinned coordinates (FR-16) and enables the field/offline usage conditions (NFR-03/NFR-07) for this round | [FR-33 AC-1](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-33 | High |
| TC-06-08 | Set a supervision round as online (new 2026-09-16) | The Supervising Instructor begins recording a supervision round for a company they are responsible for | 1. Log in with a Supervising Instructor account<br>2. Open a new supervision round for the company they are responsible for<br>3. Select the supervision mode as "online" | The system does not enforce any coordinates/route and does not enter the NFR-03/NFR-07 conditions for this round (treated as used over a normal device/internet connection) | [FR-33 AC-2](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-33 | High |
| TC-06-09 | Record scores/interview successfully in both modes (new 2026-09-16) | Supervision rounds have been set to both on-site (continuing from TC-06-07) and online (continuing from TC-06-08), 1 round each | 1. Record the rubric scores for the on-site round and the online round<br>2. Record the interview-form results for the on-site round and the online round<br>3. Open the results of both rounds | The system saves the data successfully in both cases, with the selected mode (online/onsite) always saved together with that supervision result | [FR-33 AC-3](../acceptance-criteria.md) | FR-33 | High |
| TC-06-10 | Save scores successfully despite a temporary signal drop (on-site round only) | The Supervising Instructor is recording scores/interview results via a mobile device at the host company during an on-site supervision round where the internet signal is unstable | 1. Begin filling in the scores/interview results for the on-site supervision round<br>2. Simulate a temporary internet signal drop while filling in<br>3. The signal reconnects again before leaving the screen<br>4. Click save | The entered data is not lost and is saved into the system successfully once the signal returns (this condition applies only to on-site rounds) | [NFR-03 AC-1](../acceptance-criteria.md) | NFR-03 | High |
| TC-06-11 | Keep a local draft when there is no internet signal (on-site round only) | The Supervising Instructor is recording scores/interview results via a mobile device in an on-site supervision round with no internet signal at that moment | 1. Turn off the device's internet signal<br>2. Fill in the scores/interview results for the on-site supervision round<br>3. Click save | The system keeps the data as a local draft on the device immediately, and does not delete the draft automatically no matter how long it remains pending | [NFR-07 AC-1](../acceptance-criteria.md) | NFR-07 | High |
| TC-06-12 | Auto-retry syncing the data when the signal returns (on-site round only) | There is an unsynced local draft of an on-site supervision round pending on the device (continuing from TC-06-11) | 1. Turn the device's internet signal back on so it is usable again<br>2. Wait without manually pressing sync | The system auto-retries syncing the data to the server automatically, and the data is saved into the system successfully once the sync completes | [NFR-07 AC-2](../acceptance-criteria.md) | NFR-07 | High |
| TC-06-13 | A banner warning of data pending sync (on-site round only) | There is an unsynced local draft of an on-site supervision round pending on the device (continuing from TC-06-11 before the signal returns) | 1. Open/use the app during the period when the sync has not yet succeeded | The system shows a message/banner warning that there is data pending sync throughout that period until the sync succeeds | [NFR-07 AC-3](../acceptance-criteria.md) | NFR-07 | High |
